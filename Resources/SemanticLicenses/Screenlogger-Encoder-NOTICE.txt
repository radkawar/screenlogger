Quantized model data
Downloaded data is pinned by size and SHA-256. It is hosted separately at:
https://huggingface.co/Nurymanau/EmbeddingGemma-2-MLX-Swift
Revision: 06b4e075e6b3772e3c29df9f2a2ace7bd4950bdd
All 1,290 stored text/vision tensors were compared with the original Google
checkpoint. Each of 333 packed matrices, its scales, and its biases exactly
match MLX 0.32.3 affine 8-bit conversion with groups of 64. Other tensors are
unchanged. Only the audio tower and audio projection are omitted.
The data host supplies no executable code or Swift package to Screenlogger.
